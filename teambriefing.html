Q2 Safety Campaign Focus: Enclosed Space Entry Protocols

Target Scope: All Active Marine Personnel & Shipboard Safety Officers

Analyses indicate that multi-gas verification omission remains the primary variable in deep-tank incident curves. Before activating authorization frames, all oxygen meters must undergo cross-calibration checks ashore or verified through mandatory gas calibration gas canisters.

Mandatory Pre-Entry Briefing Checklist Items:

  • Minimum 21% continuous volumetric oxygen calibration verified.
  • Dedicated rescue team standby positioned outside hatch with SCBA units primed.
  • Intrinsic safe walkie-talkie communication relay checkpoints synchronized at 5-minute segments.
